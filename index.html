Dear Bestie,

Happy 20th birthday to you. ❤️

I don't know if I will ever be able to put everything I feel into words, but today I want to try.

You know me. I am not always easy to handle. I can be stubborn, emotional, difficult, quiet, overthinking, and sometimes just plain tough. But somehow, you stayed.

You didn't just stay when I was happy. You stayed through my lows, my ups, my confusion, my bad days and my random days. You understood parts of me that I couldn't even explain properly.

No one has ever understood me and treated me the way you did.

You have done much more than what anyone would normally expect from a friend. The way you care, check on me, listen, help, tolerate my tough side and somehow know when something is wrong — all of it means more than I say.

You showed me another side of the world. You taught me that life can be happiness too. I learned so much about friendship, people, enjoying moments and seeing the good side of life from you.

These 16 months are not just time. They are conversations, laughter, chaos, memories, comfort and a bond that became much bigger than I expected.

This is your second birthday after you became part of my life, and somehow it feels like my second birthday too — because a part of the happiness I have today exists because you came into my life.

Whatever happens in life, I want you to know that I will be there. As your bestie. As your comrade. To celebrate your highs, stand beside you in your lows, annoy you, support you, listen to you and remind you who you are when you forget.

I don't promise everything will always be perfect. I promise something better — I will always choose this friendship.

Happy birthday, Dudu. ❤️
